Platform Vision & Architecture

Commercial Driver Recruitment & Staffing Platform

RouteHire connects professional commercial bus drivers with travel operators through a structured, transparent recruitment network.

The Industry Challenge

The Driver Discovery Gap

Commercial drivers often depend solely on personal contacts and individual travel companies for opportunities. Meanwhile, travel operators face immense difficulty finding experienced drivers for specific highway corridors (e.g. Nagpur → Mumbai / Hyderabad).

The RouteHire Solution

Structured Recruitment Platform

RouteHire creates a structured network where drivers showcase route experience, vehicle familiarity, and verified licences, while operators publish recruitment requirements and hire screened drivers directly.

Value Proposition

Empowering Both Sides of Transport

For Drivers

Build one professional digital profile and discover relevant recruitment opportunities matching your highway routes and vehicle expertise.

For Operators

Find and recruit reliable commercial drivers based on specific route knowledge, years of experience, and vehicle model mastery.

For RouteHire

Build a structured national professional driver network, screening pipeline, and transparent recruitment ecosystem.

Commercial Model

Platform Recruitment & Service Framework

RouteHire connects travel operators with professional drivers and facilitates recruitment. Operators pay RouteHire according to agreed recruitment or service staffing arrangements, from which RouteHire manages driver onboarding and operational margin.
